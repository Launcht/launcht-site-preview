The problem

Good ideas are not the same as good businesses.

An opportunity can be compelling without yet being commercially viable.

The challenge is knowing what to pursue, what to change, what to build and when to move.

LAUNCHT brings the disciplines required to answer those questions in sequence, reducing uncertainty before significant capital, time and execution are committed.

That sequence is what takes an opportunity to market intact. Answered out of order, the same questions produce a business that is busy rather than positioned: a product built before the demand was understood, a brand built before the position was settled, a launch date chosen because the work happened to finish.

What LAUNCHT does

From opportunity to market.

LAUNCHT is a venture-building and commercialisation platform for founders and organisations pursuing high-potential opportunities. We combine advanced market intelligence, rigorous research, strategic thinking, specialist expertise and technology-enabled execution to move an opportunity toward a business with a credible position in the market.

The process is deliberately connected.

  • Research informs strategy.
  • Strategy informs positioning.
  • Positioning informs product and brand.
  • Product and brand inform launch.
  • Market response informs what comes next.

The objective is not simply to produce a series of outputs.

It is to make better decisions, in the right sequence, with progressively greater conviction.

The process

Six phases. One connected journey.

Each phase answers a different commercial question and produces the strategic and practical inputs required for the next.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Is the opportunity worth pursuing?

    A clearer understanding of the opportunity and the questions that matter most.

  2. 02

    Validate

    Does the evidence support the conviction?

    A defensible view of whether and where the opportunity can create value.

  3. 03

    Strategise

    What should the business be, and where should it compete?

    A coherent commercial proposition with a clear position in the market.

  4. 04

    Define

    What needs to exist for the proposition to become real?

    A focused product definition designed around the opportunity, the customer and the commercial strategy.

  5. 05

    Position

    How should the business enter the market?

    A distinctive and strategically grounded brand ready to enter the market.

  6. 06

    Launch

    Is the business ready to meet the market?

    A business prepared to enter the market with clarity, coherence and purpose.

The difference

Intelligence at machine speed. Judgement where it matters.

LAUNCHT leverages advanced market intelligence methodologies, proprietary research processes and AI-enabled capabilities to accelerate the collection, analysis and synthesis of information.

But technology does not replace expertise.

How an engagement runs

Conviction. Evidence. Execution.

01

Conviction

Someone sees an opportunity others do not. An insight. An unmet need. A category shift. A new technology. A belief that something should exist.

02

Evidence

We determine whether that conviction is supported by reality. Through research, market intelligence, competitive analysis, commercial assessment and specialist expertise, we establish what the evidence tells us.

03

Execution

We translate that understanding into a business capable of entering the market. Strategy, proposition, product definition, positioning, brand and launch become connected parts of the same commercial journey.
